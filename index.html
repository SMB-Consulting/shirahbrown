About

Hi, I'm Shirah.

Good systems create clarity. The best ones make room for people.

For fifteen years I've stepped into complex, tangled work — technology, workflows, data, and the processes wrapped around them — and made it move forward with confidence.

I care as much about the people using a system as the system itself — governance that holds, platforms that scale, decisions that stick, never at the expense of the humans on the other side of the screen. Philadelphia-based; I played Division I lacrosse, which is where I learned to lead under pressure and be the reliable one when it counts.

How I work

Builder + operator = architect operator.

I envision and build complex systems, and I bring the operational discipline to execute, scale and keep improving them.

Assess & diagnose

A current-state read on the systems, the data, the workflows and who owns what, including the parts nobody has written down. Then the truth about what's working, what's costing you and what to fix first.

Architect & align

Target-state architecture across websites, CRM, engagement platforms, events, integration and identity. How the systems fit together — ownership, data flow, governance — at the scale the organization can actually absorb.

Lead & operate

Multi-million-dollar initiatives and executive reporting. I've run the programs, not just advised on them, so the roadmap gets delivered.
