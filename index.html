About

Hi, I'm Shirah.

Good systems create clarity. The best ones make room for people.

For fifteen years I've stepped into complex, tangled work — technology, workflows, data, and the processes wrapped around them — and made it move forward with confidence. I set the direction, design the architecture, and stay hands-on until it ships. I'm a systems architect and a transformation leader rather than a project manager: the work is to establish what is actually happening inside an environment, then build the operating structure that should replace it.

I care as much about the people using a system as the system itself — governance that holds, platforms that scale, decisions that stick, never at the expense of the humans on the other side of the screen. Philadelphia-based; I played Division I lacrosse, which is where I learned to lead under pressure and be the reliable one when it counts.

How I work

Builder + operator = architect operator.

I envision and build complex systems, and I bring the operational discipline to execute, scale and keep improving them.

Name what's actually happening

A current-state read on the systems, the data, the workflows, and who owns what — including the parts nobody has written down.

Design the architecture, not the tool list

Target state, sequencing, ownership, and governance at the scale the organization can actually absorb. Transformation does not require enterprise bureaucracy.

Stay until it operates

Build, integrate, document, hand off. Technology should support the operating model, not become the operating model.
