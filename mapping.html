    Questions

    Maps, wards & divisions.

    What is a walk map?

    A walk map shows a canvasser exactly which streets and blocks to cover, usually one division or precinct at a time. A good one names every street on the boundary so nobody walks the wrong side.

    What’s the difference between a ward, a division and a precinct?

    In Philadelphia, the city is split into 66 wards, and each ward into divisions; a division is the city’s word for a voting precinct. Elsewhere in Pennsylvania the same unit is usually called a precinct or district.

    Can you map my whole district?

    Yes. Ward, division and precinct boundaries come from official files, so a State House, State Senate or council district can be mapped division by division.

    Do the maps work for door-knocking teams?

    That’s what they’re for: turf by division or block, status shading, and a view that works on a phone at the door, with a clean print version for packets.
